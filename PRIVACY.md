# Friend 360 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Friend 360 is a class project (CSC 4330). This policy describes what the app does today. If that changes, this policy changes before the new behavior ships.

## What we read

With your permission, Friend 360 reads these from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android): steps, sleep, workouts and active calories. It asks only for these four. It never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.

## Where your data goes

Today the health data stays on your phone. The app has no accounts and no server, and it does not send your health data anywhere. It is used only to draw your stats and your place on the weekly leaderboard.

The friends and circles you see in the app are sample data for a demo. They are not real people. The Fitbit connection status and the sharing switches on the Sharing & privacy screen are also demo only: nothing is connected to Fitbit and nothing is shared.

## What we don't do

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. We don't use it for credit, insurance or employment decisions, and we never share it without your explicit consent. We don't write health values to logs or crash reports.

## Retention and security

No copy of your health data leaves your phone. The app keeps it only in memory while it runs, and your phone's app sandbox protects it. Uninstalling the app deletes anything it holds.

## Sleep and other private data

Sleep is private by default. Sharing any metric with other people will always be opt-in, per metric, and you can turn it off again.

## Your choices

You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app on iPhone (Sharing > Apps > Friend 360), or in Health Connect on Android (App permissions > Friend 360). Uninstalling the app removes the data it holds on your phone.

## Future changes

If we add accounts or sharing with friends, health data you choose to share would be stored on a server and shown to the people you pick. This policy will be updated first, and you will be asked before anything is shared.

## Children

Friend 360 is not directed at children under 13.
