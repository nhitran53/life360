# Friend 360 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Friend 360 is a class project (CSC 4330). This policy describes what the app does today. If that changes, this policy changes before the new behavior ships.

## What we read

With your permission, Friend 360 reads these from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android): steps, sleep, workouts and active calories. It asks only for these four. It never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect. You can also type in your screen time by hand.

## Your account

You sign in with an email and password, Google or Apple. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which keeps your email address or your Google or Apple account ID.

Your profile is a display name, a username, an emoji avatar and a friend code. Anyone signed in to Friend 360 can see your profile, so they can find and add you. Your goals and sharing settings are private to you.

## What friends can see

The app uploads your daily totals for the stats you choose to share to Google Cloud Firestore. Only you and the friends you accepted can read them. It uploads one total per stat per day, never individual readings or sleep stages, and stats you keep private are never uploaded. Sleep and screen time stay private until you turn them on.

## Changing your mind

Turning a stat off in Privacy removes it from the days already uploaded. If your phone is offline, that happens as soon as it reconnects, and until then friends may still see the last value. Pausing sharing stops uploads, deletes what was uploaded and hides your stats from friends right away. A friend you remove can no longer see your stats.

## Demo features

The circles and families on the Circle tab, nudges, notifications and the Fitbit card still use sample data. Those people aren't real, and nothing is connected to Fitbit.

## What we don't do

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. We don't share it with anyone except your accepted friends and Google Firebase, which stores it for us. We don't use it for credit, insurance or employment decisions. We don't write health values to logs or crash reports.

## Retention and security

Health readings stay on your phone, inside its app sandbox. Only the daily totals you share leave it. Firebase encrypts them in transit and at rest, and security rules let only you and your accepted friends read them. Uploaded days older than 14 days are deleted.

## Your choices

You can change or revoke health access at any time in the Health app on iPhone (Sharing > Apps > Friend 360), or in Health Connect on Android (App permissions > Friend 360). You choose what friends see under Privacy in the app.

To delete everything, open Account and tap Delete account. It removes your profile, username, friend code, friendships, goals, sharing settings and every uploaded day, then your sign-in account.

## Changes to this policy

If what the app collects or shares changes, we update this policy before the change ships and update the date at the top.

## Children

Friend 360 is not directed at children under 13.
